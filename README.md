# The World in One Day — 25 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026,
drawn as a newspaper front page. The image itself names the day and tells
the stories — no news feed needed to read it.

## The day's five big stories

1. **U.S. tariffs hit 14 nations** — Washington proposes new tariffs on 14
   major economies; China faces 20%, rising to 40% if the trade truce lapses.
   The front page charts the new rates: China 20%, Vietnam 20%, EU 15%,
   Japan 15%.
2. **Houthis strike Riyadh's tallest tower** — 11 hurt. The Houthis also
   claim an attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery, and the U.S. urges
   its citizens to leave Iran.
3. **Shenzhou-24 docks at Tiangong** — China's crew ship reaches its space
   station carrying the first foreign astronaut to fly aboard it.
4. **Cairo tanker fire kills 30** — a fuel truck overturns and catches fire
   on the ring road near Cairo; 27 vehicles burn.
5. **Judges: drop Netanyahu bribery case** — Israeli judges recommend
   prosecutors drop the most serious corruption case against the caretaker
   prime minister.

The footer ticker adds the rest of the day: Poland's no-fly zone at the
Ukraine border, Bangladesh's 36% garment-pay rise, Huawei's conviction in a
U.S. trial, Sudan drone strikes killing 19, North Korea expelling a Swiss
diplomat, and leaked data putting Russian war dead in Ukraine above 490,000.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` — the
hand-drawn 1024×1024 front page, under 1 MiB — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Deterministic: every run writes the same
bytes.
